Abandoned but not forgotten

The machines we left behind

Every piece of hardware from the challenge, plus India’s missions, in the place it rests today. Almost all of it stopped working long ago. Two Skylab repair bases, on the Moon and Mars, can fly it in, fix it and bring it back to life.

Planet sizes and distances are not to scale. Positions on the Moon and Mars are the real landing coordinates. Data: NSSDCA & NASA Science. 3D models: NASA 3D Resources. Moon: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit. Mars: USGS Viking MDIM 2.1 mosaic. Skylab bases: concept designs built from NASA and ESA studies (Artemis south-pole regions, Fission Surface Power, VSAT, Kilopower, MOXIE, Mars Ice Home, SWIM ice maps). Built for the Space Apps 2026 challenge.
